# SUARA Playground

Pick a recording of dysarthric speech, press **Play & transcribe**, and compare off-the-shelf Whisper with SUARA.

Live: https://fariz36.github.io/suara-playground/

Static front-end only: the audio samples and both recognisers' transcripts are bundled in `audio/` and `data.js` (rebuild with `tools/build_data.py`).

Audio samples are from the TORGO database (Rudzicz, Namasivayam & Wolff, 2012), used with permission.
